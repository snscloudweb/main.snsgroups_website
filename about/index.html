About SNS Institutions

SNS Institutions is a group of nine educational institutions in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India, run by Sri SNS Charitable Trust since 1997. It offers education from kindergarten to PhD across engineering, arts and science, management, pharmacy, nursing, physiotherapy, allied health sciences and teacher education, built on a Design Thinking and AI-based learning model.

SNS Institutions is the education arm of SNS Groups.

How SNS teaches

3P Culture

Purpose, Process and People: the culture that guides learning from kindergarten to PhD.

3C Competency Circle

Attitude (curiosity and accountability), Technical Skill (domain expertise) and Logical and Business Skill (problem-solving and business acumen), built into every programme.

Five pillars

Center for Learning & Teaching, Center for Creativity, Skill & Career Development, Industry Institute Partnership Cell, and Social Responsibility Initiatives (SANTA 365, the Neighborhood School Program and Talent Hunt).
